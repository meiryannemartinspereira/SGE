## Functional Requirements

1. **Product, brand, supplier, category, inflow, and outflow registration.**
2. **Product filtering.**
3. **Automatic calculation of the available stock quantity for each product**, based on the registered inflows and outflows.
4. **User login system.**
5. **User and/or group permission management**, including different access levels.
6. **Dashboards with metrics and charts** for sales, inflows, outflows, and inventory value.
7. **Support for future integrations and automations.**
